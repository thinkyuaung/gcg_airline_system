All Questions Ticketing Baggage Check-in Refunds

You can easily manage and reschedule your flight ticket through your User Booking Dashboard up to 24 hours prior to departure. Alternatively, you can contact our customer support hotline or visit our ticket office with your booking reference ID.

Standard Economy Class tickets include 7kg of cabin hand luggage and 20kg of checked baggage. Business Class passengers enjoy up to 30kg of checked baggage allowance. Extra baggage weight can be purchased during ticket booking.

Online check-in opens 24 hours before your flight's scheduled departure time and closes 2 hours before takeoff. You can download or print your electronic boarding pass directly from our portal.

Cancellations requested 48 hours prior to flight departure are eligible for an 80% refund. Refunds are automatically processed back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

All passengers must present a valid passport (valid for at least 6 months), necessary entry visas for international destinations, and their physical or digital E-ticket boarding pass.
