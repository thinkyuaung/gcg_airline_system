Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026
1. Personal Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you register an account, purchase flight tickets, or subscribe to our newsletter. This includes:

  • Passenger full name, date of birth, passport information, and nationality.
  • Contact details such as email address and phone number.
  • Payment information and transaction history (secured via SSL encryption).
2. How Information is Used

Your data helps us facilitate flight reservations, verify passenger identity, issue electronic boarding passes, and send important flight status updates or notifications.

3. Data Protection Standards

Maubin AirLine employs bank-grade security protocols to store and safeguard your personal details against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure.

Terms of Service

Updated General Conditions
1. Flight Booking & Ticketing

All ticket reservations made through our web application are subject to availability and identity confirmation. E-tickets must match the exact passenger details as printed on valid government passport IDs.

2. Cancellations and Refunds

Flight tickets can be altered or cancelled under the following guidelines:

  • Cancellations requested 48+ hours prior to departure receive an 80% refund.
  • Modifications within 24 hours of flight departure are subject to a standard re-booking charge.
3. Luggage Allowance

Standard Economy tickets permit 7kg of carry-on hand baggage and up to 20kg of checked luggage unless specified otherwise in special promotional packages.
