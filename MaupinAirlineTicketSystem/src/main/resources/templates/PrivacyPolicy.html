Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. Learn how Maubin AirLine collects, uses, and safeguards your personal flight data.

1. Information Collection

Updated July 2026

We collect personal details essential for completing flight reservations, processing electronic boarding tickets, and complying with civil aviation regulations. This includes:

  • Passenger Identity: Full Name, Date of Birth, Passport Number, and Nationality.
  • Contact Information: Phone Number, Physical Address, and Email Address.
  • Payment Records: Encrypted transaction IDs and billing records.

2. Data Security & Storage

High-Level Encryption

We utilize SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption for all data transfers. Your payment details and identity files are stored in protected database environments subject to restricted access controls.

3. Information Sharing

Aviation & Legal Compliance

Maubin AirLine does not sell or rent customer data to marketing third parties. Data is only shared with official immigration authorities, airport security control, or payment gateway providers necessary to complete your travel itinerary.
